Leonida Trophy Guide

Every one of the 55 trophies, with a guide to each, a roadmap to the platinum, and a checklist that remembers your progress. Works fully offline.

Watch out in Chapter 1: One trophy may be missable — Positive Mental Attitude. On the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. That moment is gone after Chapter 1.

Road to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no difficulty settings, no online trophies. Cheats switch trophies off for the session, so keep them away from your trophy run.

  1. 1

    Play the story

    Five chapters. Take side content as it shows up, scan vehicles with the WAiNK app, study animals with binoculars, and meditate with both characters. Do not skip Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1.

  2. 2

    Finish the 100% checklist

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Robberies, bank vaults, gang operations, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s Target Decks — the longest stretch, around 100 hours.

  3. 3

    Strangers & clean-up

    Finish the stranger strands and character quest lines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), then sweep up whatever is left on the list below.

What 100% completion asks for

The A Long Road gold trophy. Everything you did before the credits already counts.

  • All robberies, including the 8 bank vaults
  • All 39 gang operations
  • Every underwater discovery
  • Gold in every Shooting Range challenge
  • All drone races
  • All 15 of Bret’s Target Decks
  • 100 optional missions
  • 200 of 277 vehicle scans
  • 100 of 255 animal studies
  • 15 of 18 meditations
  • 70 of 80 challenges
  • 300 stunts · 100 WAiNK scans
